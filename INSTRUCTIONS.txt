YEMA / MEMBER IN-GAME DEPOSITS

Setup:
1. Offshore config must have banker_nation_id = Yema (633226).
2. Keys: BANK_API_KEY (main), OFFSHORE_API_KEY (Yema), etc.
3. Yema must be an active member on the site (logged in once) to get site balance credit.
4. Optional: econ_settings key offshore_deposit_nation_ids = [633226] for extra names.

What sync does:
- Main AA bankrecs: nation→alliance → credit that member's website deposit.
- Offshore AA bankrecs: only Yema (banker) / listed nations → credit THEIR website deposit
  AND add the same amounts to Evergreen net-at-offshore books (offshore_evergreen_credit).

Run:
- Cron: GET /api/cron/bank-deposits?secret=CRON_SECRET
- Staff: POST /api/gov/sync-deposits

Other alliances depositing into the same offshore are IGNORED (not Evergreen books).
